Further up38 optimizations — research report

Conclusion
Five additional simplifications have been implemented. Together they reduce CPU cycles for the complete rendering pipeline by 1.51–4.88% in the tested scenes versus up38. This compares identical scene states; it is NOT a new whole-game speedup percentage. Limited flight benchmarks do not yet establish a meaningful FPS gain. The candidate remains experimental.

Scope and identity
Baseline: supplied up38 ROM, SHA256 6087e9b314368b95e7885f5634c88a506bbc61d7a4c988db345e7373377253da.
Candidate: sentinel_depth_lazy_scan_fill.rom / .car.
ROM SHA256: ec01982121b70baca35f04568617965c3164e716e0a5eb3c6ebcbb123566a868.
Experiment source: scripts/research_up38.py. Generated assembly, symbol files and binaries are alongside this report. The supplied package, repository directory and published release were not modified. No corresponding XEX or official release was prepared.

Profile
Across 40 scenes: 114777 CPU cycles per pipeline on average. Bank 5 rasterization: 61609 (53.7%); bank 3 projection: 19005; bank 2: 15466; bank 9 fill: 10185; banks 6/8: 6912; bank 14 prologue: 1575. This motivated prioritizing the plotter and its clearing/filling helpers.

Changes
1. Represent a fully cleared column with HD=152 instead of 151.
The maximum plotted height is 151. Previously HD=151 triggered lazy clear at that height, although the helper immediately returned without clearing. The new sentinel avoids these calls without adding per-pixel instructions. Dispatch tables, initialization and both helpers were updated. Object drawing can still produce 151; it remains supported rather than being assumed unreachable.
2. Restore Y once at the shared path exit.
In d0–d4 specializations, remove LDY after individual pixels and replace the shared DEY with LDY of a known constant. This does not modify general recursion dg or every specialized leaf. It removes redundant instructions without changing subdivision.
3. Perform lazy clear directly in the HD/h coordinate domain.
Reverse jump tables and comparisons remove conversions of both heights into row numbers. Clearing-boundary rounding and HD=151/152 handling remain equivalent. Applies to banks 5 and 8.
4. Keep the occlusion threshold in the accumulator.
In specialized scans o0–o4, replace four LDA/CMP pairs with four memory CMP instructions. Use Hmax-1 and reverse the failure condition; advance X with four INX instructions to preserve A. These paths handle widths of at least 5, so their positive bound contribution guarantees Hmax>0. This was not applied unconditionally to the generic path, where Hmax=0 would need separate handling.
5. Fill directly uses HD instead of converting to D=152-HD.
Reverse table indexing and comparisons remove subtraction from dispatch and sky-band handling, preserving image contents.

Validation
- 320 recorded scenes: identical image, relevant RAM, A/X/Y/SP, live stack and semantically normalized clearing-boundary cache.
- 5000 randomized segments, including widths 1–128 and screen edges: identical image buffers and occlusion table; seed 20261029. This test does not require identical dead registers/scratch RAM after each segment.
- 300 object-plotter calls: identical relevant RAM and A/X/Y/SP.
- 160 successive states from a natively booted candidate, 80 each PAL/NTSC: neutral flight followed by random steering/firing. Replaying up38 with equivalent inputs produced identical images/relevant RAM/registers. Candidate sentinel 152 is converted to equivalent baseline value 151 when preparing baseline inputs.
- For these 160 states, differing processor flags were tracked to their first read/write: none was consumed before being overwritten.
- Three Esc PAUSE/RESUME cycles per video standard, with five presentations after each resume: passed.
- Two clearing helpers: 11880 cases (5940 per bank), every valid HD/h combination that invokes the helper, six column positions, identical image, cache semantics and X/Y/SP. No differences.
- Beam-synchronized sampling of 100 PAL and 100 NTSC frames found no image changes in a buffer still being displayed.
Details: *-comparison.json, *-fuzz.json, *-objects.json and *-native.json. Beam-synchronization results are in *-tearing.json; helper results are in *-helpers.json.
This is neither a proof covering every gameplay situation nor a hardware test. XEX, accelerators and every landing/pilot/alien/game-over scene were not validated.

Limited native cold-boot FPS benchmark
Preselected trials 4, 78 and 154 per standard, up to 60 seconds each, using four isolated emulator processes. Same B516 presentation measurement and 170-frame warmup methodology as before. All six entry RAM hashes matched up38. Arithmetic means of three trials:
PAL: up38 9.244679; candidate 9.219327; difference -0.025352 FPS (-0.2742%).
NTSC: up38 8.407107; candidate 8.444859; difference +0.037752 FPS (+0.4490%).
Individual differences range from -0.407687 to +0.301633 FPS.
These are not new reliable game averages and do not replace the 155-flight series. Lower cost for identical scenes is confirmed; a higher whole-game mean FPS is NOT established by this test. Execution timing changes can alter subsequent flight behavior; DMA/VBI and synchronization are also excluded from the cycle table. No diagnostic established the specific cause of each FPS discrepancy. Do not advertise a new +X% result based on this experiment.
An earlier sentinel_depth_lazy candidate also received six flights; its results remain in a separate JSON file. Total: 12 measurement flights, not a full series.

Rejected variants
- Proactively clear one extra row: only 2049.95 cycles saved over 40 scenes, versus 2492.125 for the sentinel alone. LUT/per-column overhead outweighed reduced subsequent work. This does not rule out every predictive-clearing algorithm.
- Defer STX $82 until subdivision: tested in sentinel_store_fill, correct across 320 scenes, but its small and uneven effect did not justify inclusion in the selected candidate.

Further directions, without promised gains
- Specializing buffer-address tables could eliminate some prologue work, but terrain and object paths need a consistent solution and revised bank layout. Simply removing table rewriting would be incorrect.
- Projection still costs around 19000 cycles. Division is already unrolled and heavily optimized; reciprocal/table methods need exact remainder/overflow correction to preserve bitwise results. No faster working implementation or measured gain exists here yet.
- Tighter phase-dependent culling bounds might eliminate more falsely visible subtrees; this needs a bound proof and cost analysis. Not implemented.
None of the five working changes requires additional banks or expanded RAM.
